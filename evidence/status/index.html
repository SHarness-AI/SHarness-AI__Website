What exists today, and what does not

The honest column, as of 25 Oct 2026. SHarness is at the design stage with the first experiments runnable. What exists as running code is listed here and kept apart from the design, so a reader, human or agent, can tell what they can rely on.

Exists, and can be checked

ThingWhere to check it
The sharness-ai package on PyPI, v0.1.0: reports its version and prints its helppypi.org/project/sharness-ai
Experiments E01 to E04 and E10 (probe, policy hash, run, hello, policy translate), with 40 tests, on the CLI's dev branch; unreleased to PyPIthe code · experiments/README.md · E04 measured on Evidence §9
Two example policies and a skill for Claude Code sessionsExamples
Two browser mockups that compute and enforce nothingthe policy hash (the same hash as the CLI) · the edge matrix
Three storyboards, data drawn by the storyboards.sgit.ai kitStoryboards; what they show is the design, not something built
The design, published for review, with the brief and four drawn diagramsThe brief · the source documents, verbatim

Not here yet

MissingStatus
Any SHarness component (launcher, kms, net, fs, bash, llm)Not built. Designed on Components.
A CLI release beyond --versionv0.1.0 on PyPI is the skeleton and the pipeline; the experiments are on dev, unreleased (R7).
The launcher above the agentNot built. Every experiment applies a policy to a child process; nothing yet binds the agent itself.
The process-tree proof of concept (E06)Started, not completed, not run. See Enforcing the process tree.
seccomp, the cpu cgroup, read-only bindsAvailable in the measured kernel; not yet applied by the CLI or measured (memory and pids caps are, since 25 Oct 2026). See Containment.
The newsroom's agents' policiesThe second user in the memo; their file and network behaviour policies are not written.
