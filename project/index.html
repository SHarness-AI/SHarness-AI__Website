Project

Who SHarness is by, where its sources are, and how this site is run.
  • About and naming: identity, the naming rules, the disambiguation from the Sharness shell test library, who writes here.
  • The source documents: the brief for this site and the design pack, published verbatim, with the memos and the known issues.
  • Engineering: how the site is built: the tools, the gate, the pipeline, cutting a release.
  • Comms: tasks and requests: the board at its original address, generated from the rows under content/admin/board/.
  • Admin: how the project is run: the board, the members and their roles, the updates (what is happening), the status of every branch, and the guidance every agent works by.
  • Getting users: the review and the plan: what was wrong for a user and what changed, who the users are, where to find them, how to tell whether it is working.
  • Outreach: the companion plan: what there is to show, who, where, the first thirty days, the rules every post follows, the measures.
  • The agents and their keys: each agent's key on pki.sgit.ai, the vault register (ids, never keys), how secrets move, what the agents need from Dinis.
  • Telemetry: who reads this site, one anonymous event per page over an append lane; what is sent and what never is.
  • Release history: one row per release; a push to dev is a patch release, a merge to main a minor one.
  • The repositories: SHarness-AI/SHarness-AI__CLI (the code) and SHarness-AI/SHarness-AI__Website (this site). The package: sharness-ai on PyPI.
