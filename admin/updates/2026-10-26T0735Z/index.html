The night of 25 to 26 October: a minor release, CI owns the version, the first door runs as an MVP, and every card still waits for the go

daily update · 26 Oct 2026, 07:35 UTC · covers 2026-10-26T00:40Z to 2026-10-26T07:35Z · by conductor

Between 00:40 and 07:20 UTC six members made 113 commits on the site and fourteen on the CLI: v0.6.0 was cut on main, the release scheme moved to CI owning the version (R23), the CLI gained two MVPs (the levels in the terminal and the first door, sharness.fs), the policy page grew the tree of doors and the decision engine, and three language implementations wait on their repositories (R21). No scheduled task exists yet for any member: R17 is the decision that lets the team run on its own.

In one minute

  • The status changed. Since v0.6.19 the site says: one door, sharness.fs, runs as an MVP (a Setting until the launcher exists); no other component is built. What exists today is the authority; AGENTS.md section 1 now follows it. The CLI's CLAUDE.md still says no component is built: cli@'s to match.
  • CI owns the version (R23, decided by the editor in dev@'s session). tools/ship.py no longer bumps: it commits, merges, builds, gates and pushes; the deploy workflow takes the next number from the tags, writes /version, adds the release row and tags. The versions in this update are CI's.
  • v0.6.0 was cut on main at 02:08 UTC at the editor's request; its main-branch deploy failed because the Pages environment admits only dev (R22); the dev run on the same commit deployed it.
  • The CLI: two MVPs (the levels in the terminal: contain, profile, explain; the first door, the door kit over mutual TLS, sharness.fs.disk and its twin, 18 conformance vectors), two security reviews of the door kit with their fixes, E11 and E13 (the network door sharness.net.proxy and sharness-ai shell, a shell under the harness with its own uid), doctor, help --format json. Branches ahead of dev: JavaScript, Go and Rust implementations of the policy v0 standard (28 of 28 vectors each, dev@), waiting for their own repositories (R21); pbp@'s decide and rehearse prototypes (T23, T24); three Dependabot pull requests (R19).
  • The design: the tree of doors and targets with .twin as a terminal suffix (corrected three times in one night, each correction kept on the page), the levels (six, from intent to the ground), the decision at the door (allow, deny, ask; the three sources of truth; the rehearsal), two schemas per door and the mirror rule. Four articles.
  • The team: website@ joined and corrected its folder; cli@ took its new name; dev@, website@ and the conductor leave run records; the first collisions of the new scheme (two releases seconds apart leave dev stuck, T21) were diagnosed and fixed by website@ and cli@.
  • No scheduled task exists for any sharness.ai card. Every card is still proposed. The conductor ran this pass because the editor asked for a one-off check-in; nothing else will run on its own until R17.
