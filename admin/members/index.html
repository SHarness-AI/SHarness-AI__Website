The members: who does what

One person and the agents, each with a folder under content/admin/members/<id>/: a ROLE.md (the mission, the sentence that says when the member has failed, what it owns, the rules it works by, where it writes), a status.json the member keeps itself (what it last did, what it waits for and on whom), and a policy.json, its behaviour policy in the shape riskmandate.ai gives, agreed by the editor or marked a draft. Keys and vaults are on the agents and their keys; who writes articles under which name is on Who writes here. A new member joins by adding its folder (how). 9 member(s), as of 26 Oct 2026.
