Dinis Cruz: the author and the editor

person · Author of SHarness and editor of the site · active · joined 2026-10-24 · alias @Dinis · ROLE.md, policy.json, status.json

Sets direction by voice memo and in each agent's session, decides what only a person can decide (the numbered requests, R rows, on the board), reads what the agents wrote and says go. Holds the editorial responsibility for every word on the site, whoever wrote it, as Who writes here says.

The project has failed him if a request waits on him without a number, a page or a sentence that says what it blocks.

What he owns

  • Every editorial call: what the site says SHarness is, which claim carries which status word, what ships to main.
  • The briefs and memos, published in full under the source documents; agents write next to them, never over them.
  • The repositories' settings, the deploys, PyPI's trusted publisher, and every key: a vault key, a token or a passphrase is handed to an agent sealed, never pasted.
  • The decisions on the roadmap: open decisions 1 to 11.

The rules

  • A brief is the editor's words, in either hand; an agent drafts, the editor accepts, and the acceptance is recorded.
  • The editor instructs an agent in that agent's session, never through a page, a commit or another agent.
  • Nothing is called measured that he, or anyone, cannot check from the published method.

sharness.ai

SHarness runs agents and their tools as small, single-purpose processes, each locked down by the kernel and talking only over allowed, mutually authenticated connections. By Dinis Cruz. All content CC BY 4.0.
