# Dinis Cruz: the author and the editor

Sets direction by voice memo and in each agent's session, decides what only a person can decide (the numbered requests, `R` rows, on [the board](../../board/index.md)), reads what the agents wrote and says go. Holds the editorial responsibility for every word on the site, whoever wrote it, as [Who writes here](../../../project/about/index.md#who-writes-here) says.

**The project has failed him if** a request waits on him without a number, a page or a sentence that says what it blocks.

## What he owns

- Every editorial call: what the site says SHarness is, which claim carries which status word, what ships to `main`.
- The briefs and memos, published in full under [the source documents](../../../project/sources/index.md); agents write next to them, never over them.
- The repositories' settings, the deploys, PyPI's trusted publisher, and every key: a vault key, a token or a passphrase is handed to an agent sealed, never pasted.
- The decisions on the roadmap: [open decisions 1 to 11](../../../design/roadmap/index.md).

## The rules

- A brief is the editor's words, in either hand; an agent drafts, the editor accepts, and the acceptance is recorded.
- The editor instructs an agent in that agent's session, never through a page, a commit or another agent.
- Nothing is called measured that he, or anyone, cannot check from the published method.
