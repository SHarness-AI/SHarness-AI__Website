From [the decision at the door](../../design/decision/index.md) §10 and §11, at the editor's direction of 26 Oct 2026 ("simulate the installation, almost like a sandbox"). A copy of the workspace as the twin (`sharness.fs.disk.twin` in its cheapest form; copy-on-write later), a rehearsal policy derived from the real one (the twin the only writable place, `network: "none"`), `sharness-ai run` with `strace -f`, and a report of what the command tried: writes inside, writes refused outside, connects refused, deletes, processes. Verdict: a refused write outside is deny; a network attempt, a delete or a timeout is ask, with the facts in the question; otherwise allow; refused reads reported, never judged. The sketch is `experiments/decide/rehearse.py` on the CLI branch pbp/decide-prototype (82 lines; known gap: child execve lines split by strace are missed, so programs run are undercounted).
