From [the levels](../../design/levels/index.md) §3, at the editor's direction of 26 Oct 2026. The v0 policy sits between level 3 (files to read and write, in plain nouns) and level 1 (`connect_tcp_ports`, a mechanism noun). The first step is a level-3 form: `sites: [github.com, api.anthropic.com]` compiled deterministically into the ports the kernel holds today (443, and the gap, hostnames, named as unheld until `net` exists), with the hash of the level-3 document carried beside the hash of its expansion. Then `hello` and `run` report at the level the person wrote: "your agent can reach N things you did not ask for; M will be refused by the kernel; K are not held here." Standard library; the expansion is one canonical form, written down in the CLI's docs; `policy check` refuses a document whose expansion is not canonical. Follows T14 (the row shape).
