From [the decision at the door](../../design/decision/index.md) §4 and §7, at the editor's direction of 26 Oct 2026. The engine maps a shell command to the rows of the policy the process runs under and answers allow (every row held), deny (a row refused, a behaviour row broken, or a command it cannot read: unknown is no) or ask (a row the policy cannot hold), with a one-line summary in UX-059's shape (reads, writes, network, reversible) and, for ask, a question in the person's words with three choices (once, add the row, refuse). Deterministic: the same command under the same policy gets the same answer. Intent is carried into the question and never changes the verdict. The verdict carries the harness's PreToolUse hook form, so it can sit behind a hook today (a Setting) and inside sharness.bash later (a Boundary). A 99-line prototype with twelve sample commands is on the CLI repository's branch pbp/decide-prototype under experiments/decide/, to take or replace; every verdict needs a test. Pairs with T22 (the level-3 form, so that "ask" for a host becomes a row the policy can hold).
