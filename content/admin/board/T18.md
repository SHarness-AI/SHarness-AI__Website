Decided by the editor in pbp's session on 26 Oct 2026 and written on [the PBP on one page](../../design/pbp/index.md) §6: a door is `sharness.<resource>` (`fs`, `net`, `db`, `llm`, `bash`, `kms`); a dialect is `sharness.<resource>.<dialect>` (`sharness.db.sqlite`, `sharness.net.http`, `sharness.net.git`, `sharness.fs.s3`, `sharness.llm.tools`) and sits on its parent, which closes every other path; depth is open and a level earns a name only when it adds nouns its parent cannot see. The naming rules on [About and naming](../../project/about/index.md) say `sharness.<role>` with seven names; they should carry the tree, and the code tree with it (`sharness_ai.db.sqlite`; a distribution `sharness-ai-db-sqlite` if a dialect leaves the CLI). For the conductor as ontologist; the component pages that follow from it are build@sharness's.
