From [R22](index.md#r22), the editor's words of 26 Oct 2026, the same as the `git__increment-tag` action's: **major** is the release a push to `main` makes, bumping the middle number and zeroing the last; **minor** is the release a push to `dev` makes, bumping the last number. Never *patch* for the one or *minor* for the other. website@sharness carries the words into the comments of `.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml` (today "a minor on main", "after a minor"), `tools/release.py`'s docstring, the releases page and `AGENTS.md` §3, and cli@sharness into the CLI's `workflow.yml` comments; the brief [CI owns the version](../briefs/2026-10-26__brief__ci-owns-the-version/index.md) and the [article](../../articles/ci-owns-the-version.html) already say it. Updates and rows already written keep their words.
