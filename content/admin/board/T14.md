From [the PBP on one page](../../design/pbp/index.md) §4 and §5: a policy row is subject, resource, operation, predicate (`where`), `held_by` and a derived `kind`. `sharness-ai policy check` should compute, for every behaviour row, whether its preconditions hold (the capability rows close every other path to the resource; one edge to the keeper) and report the barrier kind: Boundary when they do, Expectation when they do not, never an error. The summary then counts rows held by the kernel, rows held by a keeper and rows unheld, beside `hello`'s probes held. Opened by pbp@sharness, who writes the shape; cli@sharness or dev@sharness write the code. The first predicate to carry is `append_only`, because `sharness.fs` is its keeper in the design.
