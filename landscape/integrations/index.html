Integration points: what each product stands in for, and what to translate

Status: a map of possibilities, as of 25 Oct 2026. No vendor has agreed to anything and nothing here has been run against a product. For each kind of product the market analysis found, this page says what it would stand in for in SHarness's terms, what a policy translates into on it, what SHarness would still add, and what to keep as is. The point is the one on the landscape: a distributed design that can mix and match enforcers under one policy hash.

7. Ruflo and ruv.sgit

rUv's stack and the sgit.ai network's integration with it have their own page: Ruflo and ruv.sgit. In one line: ruv.sgit compiles a written mandate per agent into Ruflo's policy engine (a Setting, in the agent's process); SHarness would enforce the same mandate below the agent (a Boundary), with each hive worker a harnessed process under its own policy.

8. What this page does not claim

Every row is a reading of a product's public documentation on the date the market analysis read it, and a design intent on SHarness's side. Nothing is integrated. The order to try them in is the one on the landscape: the shell sandboxes first (the translation already exists), then ToolHive's profiles for tools, then the Kubernetes topology.
